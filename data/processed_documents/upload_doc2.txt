RENTAL AGREEMENT

(SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT FOR TESTING — FICTITIOUS PARTIES)

This Rental Agreement is made on 1st July 2026 between Mr. Joseph Kurian, owner of House No. 14, Pattom, Thiruvananthapuram (the "Landlord"), and Ms. Anjali Nair (the "Tenant").

1. Term

The Landlord lets the house to the Tenant for a period of eleven (11) months starting from 1st July 2026.

2. Rent

The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 18,000 on or before the 5th of every month. The Landlord may increase the rent at any time at his sole discretion.

3. Security Deposit

The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of Rs. 1,80,000 (ten months' rent). The deposit is non-refundable if the Tenant leaves before the end of the term.

4. Lock-in Period

There is a lock-in period of six (6) months. If the Tenant vacates during the lock-in period, the entire security deposit shall be forfeited.

5. Access

The Landlord may enter the premises at any time without notice for inspection.

6. Termination

The Landlord may terminate this agreement by giving fifteen (15) days' notice. The Tenant shall give two (2) months' notice before vacating.

7. Liability

The Tenant shall be responsible for all repairs and for any and all damages to the premises, including structural repairs.

8. Dispute Resolution

Any dispute shall be settled by the courts at Thiruvananthapuram.
