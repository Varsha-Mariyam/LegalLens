Title: Rent, revision of rent and rent receipts
Jurisdiction: India
Topic: rent
Sources: Model Tenancy Act, 2021; Kerala Buildings (Lease and Rent Control) Act, 1965
Note: Educational summary prepared for the LegalLens academic project. The Model Tenancy Act, 2021 is a model law; it applies only in States that have adopted it. Other States apply their own rent control law. Verify the law of the State where the property is located.

Under the Model Tenancy Act, 2021, rent is payable as agreed between the landlord and tenant in the tenancy agreement, and any revision of rent is to follow the terms of the agreement. The tenant is entitled to a receipt for rent paid.

States that have not adopted the Model Tenancy Act continue to apply their own rent control legislation, such as the Kerala Buildings (Lease and Rent Control) Act, 1965 in Kerala, which contains its own provisions on fair rent and eviction.

Practical reading: a rent clause should state the monthly amount, the due date, the mode of payment, what charges are included (maintenance, water, electricity), and how and when rent may be increased. A clause allowing the landlord to increase rent at any time at his sole discretion is one-sided.
