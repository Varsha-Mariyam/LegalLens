NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT
(SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT FOR TESTING — FICTITIOUS PARTIES)

This Non-Disclosure Agreement is entered into between Periyar Robotics Private Limited (the "Disclosing Party") and Bluewave Consulting (the "Receiving Party").

1. Confidential Information
Confidential Information means all information of any kind disclosed by the Disclosing Party, whether oral, written or electronic, whether or not marked confidential.

2. Obligations of the Receiving Party
The Receiving Party shall not disclose the Confidential Information to any third party and shall use it only to evaluate a possible software development project.

3. Term
The obligations of the Receiving Party shall continue indefinitely and shall survive termination of this Agreement.

4. Non-Solicitation and Non-Compete
The Receiving Party shall not provide services to any competitor of the Disclosing Party for a period of three (3) years after the termination of this Agreement.

5. Remedies
In case of any breach, the Receiving Party shall pay liquidated damages of Rs. 25,00,000 to the Disclosing Party, in addition to any other remedy.

6. Termination
The Disclosing Party may terminate this Agreement at any time without notice.

7. Dispute Resolution
The Receiving Party waives any right to approach any court. All disputes shall be decided by an arbitrator nominated by the Disclosing Party.
