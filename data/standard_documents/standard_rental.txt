PROJECT REFERENCE TEMPLATE — RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT
(Written for the LegalLens academic project. Not an official legal standard.)

1. Premises and Term
The Landlord lets the residential premises described in the Schedule to the Tenant for a term of eleven (11) months from the commencement date, renewable by mutual written agreement.

2. Rent
The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 15,000 (Rupees Fifteen Thousand only) on or before the 5th day of each month by bank transfer. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment. Rent may be revised only on renewal and only by mutual written agreement.

3. Security Deposit
The Tenant shall pay a refundable security deposit of Rs. 30,000, equal to two (2) months' rent. The Landlord shall refund the deposit within fifteen (15) days of the Tenant handing over vacant possession, after deducting only unpaid rent, unpaid utility charges and the cost of repairing damage beyond normal wear and tear, with an itemised statement.

4. Maintenance and Access
The Tenant shall keep the premises in good condition. The Landlord shall carry out structural repairs. The Landlord may enter the premises for inspection or repairs at a reasonable time after giving at least twenty-four (24) hours' prior written notice to the Tenant.

5. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving one (1) month's written notice to the other. There is no lock-in period. The Landlord shall not cut off water, electricity or other essential supplies to the premises.

6. Liability
The Tenant shall be liable only for damage to the premises caused by the Tenant's negligence or misuse, beyond normal wear and tear. Neither party shall be liable for indirect loss.

7. Dispute Resolution
Disputes shall first be discussed in good faith. Unresolved disputes shall be decided by the competent Rent Authority, Rent Court or civil court having jurisdiction over the premises.
