PROJECT REFERENCE TEMPLATE — EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT
(Written for the LegalLens academic project. Not an official legal standard.)

1. Appointment and Duties
The Employee is appointed to the position stated in the offer letter and shall perform the duties reasonably assigned by the Employer that are consistent with that position. The Employee shall report to the manager named by the Employer.

2. Salary and Payment
The Employer shall pay the Employee a monthly salary of Rs. 50,000 (Rupees Fifty Thousand only), subject to statutory deductions, on or before the 7th day of the following month by bank transfer. Any other deduction shall be made only with the written consent of the Employee or as permitted by law.

3. Working Hours and Leave
Working hours, holidays and leave shall follow the Employer's leave policy and applicable law. Earned leave shall accrue as stated in the leave policy.

4. Confidentiality
The Employee shall keep confidential all non-public business information of the Employer received during employment and shall use it only for the Employer's business. This obligation does not apply to information that is publicly available, already known to the Employee, independently developed or required to be disclosed by law. The obligation continues for two years after employment ends, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets. On leaving, the Employee shall return all Employer materials.

5. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving thirty (30) days' written notice to the other, or salary in lieu of the notice period. The Employer may terminate immediately for proven misconduct after giving the Employee a fair opportunity to be heard. All earned salary and dues shall be paid within the time required by law after termination.

6. Liability
The Employee shall be liable only for loss directly caused by the Employee's wilful misconduct or gross negligence, and such liability shall not exceed three (3) months' salary. Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential loss.

7. Dispute Resolution
The parties shall first try to resolve any dispute through good-faith discussion for thirty (30) days. Unresolved disputes shall be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed by mutual agreement of both parties under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, or failing agreement, as provided in that Act.

8. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at the place where the Employee works shall have jurisdiction subject to the arbitration clause.
